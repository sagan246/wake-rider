Support

Quick fixes

  • If steering or throttle feels stuck after switching apps, return to the game; mobile controls reset safely when the app is backgrounded.
  • If the display is cropped, rotate the device once or fully close and reopen the app.
  • Use Reset to start a fresh run without changing Physics Lab settings.

Report a problem

Open an issue in the Wake Rider Lab support tracker. Include your device model, operating-system version, app version, and a short description of what happened.

Safety

Wake Rider Lab is a game and experimental physics simulation. It is not real-world boating or water-sports training.
