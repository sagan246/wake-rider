LOCAL COORDINATES
All polygon vertices are [x east in meters, y south in meters].
Origin is longitude -122.6795, latitude 45.412, in the eastern main lake.
Projection uses x=(longitude - originLongitude) * 111320*cos(45.410 degrees), y=(originLatitude - latitude) * 111320.
This intentionally compact local equirectangular projection preserves the recognizable lake shape and is appropriate for this game footprint, not precision surveying.
All rings are closed (first point repeats as last).
