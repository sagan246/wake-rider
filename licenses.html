Lake shoreline

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

The lake boundary and five islands are derived from OpenStreetMap relation 4046990, retrieved September 30, 2026. Geometry is simplified and projected into local meters, then converted to game units at real scale. Trees and houses are decorative.

Derived geometry: download the map data. Source and processing notes.
