About this app

The shared lake has room for up to 12 players. Each player has their own colored boat and tube. Everyone sees the same wakes: cross a friend’s wake to lift the boat or launch your tube. Open Water is solo. No account is needed.

The minimap starts on and follows your boat. Edge arrows point to farther players. Set your skipper name, hide the minimap, or open the whole-lake chart in Physics → Lake map. Reset finds a clear nearby launch spot. Boats bump each other. Shared wakes keep spreading and fading after a boat passes; your boat and tube react to those waves.

The boat and rider are fictional. This remains an experimental game, not a real-world boating model.

Joining the shared lake…

Bots cruise near the launch, tow tubes, and make shared, rideable wakes. They leave when you leave. Limit 3 across the lake; real players get priority. Follows your boat with a 1 km-wide view.
